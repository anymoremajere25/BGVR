### CHAPTER 4: Data Structures and Algorithms for Genomics

#### 4.3 Motif Discovery and Regulatory Element Identification  
This section explores computational methods used to uncover recurring sequence patterns, or motifs, that play a crucial role in gene regulation. Techniques such as MEME and Gibbs sampling help detect overrepresented motifs in DNA sequences, while Position Weight Matrices (PWMs) are used to model binding site preferences. Tools like ChIP-Seq are introduced for identifying transcription factor binding sites, and the section also touches on machine learning approaches, including deep learning, that enhance motif prediction accuracy and uncover complex regulatory elements.

#### 4.6 Single-Cell Functional Genomics  
This section introduces the analysis of single-cell RNA sequencing (scRNA-Seq) data to study gene expression at the individual cell level. Key topics include normalization, dimensionality reduction (PCA, t-SNE, UMAP), and clustering techniques for identifying distinct cell types. It also covers trajectory analysis tools like Monocle to track cellular development, and discusses how integrating multi-modal data (e.g., scATAC-seq, CITE-seq) provides deeper insights into cellular functions and regulatory mechanisms at single-cell resolution.
